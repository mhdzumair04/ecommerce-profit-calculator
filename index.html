Frequently Asked Questions

What is an e-commerce profit calculator?

An e-commerce profit calculator is a tool that estimates how much profit remains after product costs, shipping, platform fees, and advertising expenses are deducted from the selling price.

What is profit margin?

Profit margin is the percentage of the selling price that remains as profit after the calculated costs are deducted.

What is ROAS?

ROAS stands for Return on Ad Spend. It compares advertising-generated revenue with advertising expenditure.

What is a break-even price?

The break-even price is the approximate selling price required to cover the costs included in this calculator.
