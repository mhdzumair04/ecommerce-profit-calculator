What Is a Break-Even Price?

Your break-even price is the minimum amount you need to charge for a product so that your revenue covers your product cost, shipping, advertising expenses, and percentage-based platform fees.

How It Works

Enter your costs and platform fee percentage above. The calculator determines the selling price where your total revenue equals your total costs.

Example

If your product costs $10, shipping costs $3, advertising costs $2, and your platform charges a 10% fee, the calculator will determine the selling price required to cover all of those costs.
