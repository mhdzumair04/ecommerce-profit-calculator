Profit Per 100 Sales Calculator

This calculator estimates the revenue and profit generated from 100 product sales.

Enter the selling price, product cost, shipping, platform fee, and advertising cost for one sale. The calculator then multiplies the results by 100.

Example

If you sell a product for $50 and make $15 profit from each sale, 100 sales would generate approximately $1,500 in profit before any additional expenses not included in the calculator.

Why calculate profit per 100 sales?

Looking at profit across 100 sales can make it easier to understand how your product economics scale with increased sales volume.
